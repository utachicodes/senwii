How a session works // 02

The relay holds no game state: it's a dumb, low-latency switchboard.

[ 01 ]

Open the room

Load SenWii on the TV or PC browser. It generates a join code and QR instantly, no sign-up.

[ 02 ]

Scan in

Each player scans with their phone camera. No install: the page itself becomes the remote.

[ 03 ]

Point and play

Point at a game to select it, press A to launch. From there it's swinging, aiming, tilting, for real.
